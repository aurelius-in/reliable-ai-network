Make it RAIN

You built something real. Now it's time to get paid. Make it RAIN.

Find who may pay, stress-test the offer, and figure out the next conversation worth having.

Who it is for

Software creators who have shipped an app, SaaS product, AI tool, or working MVP, and still cannot answer the commercial questions with evidence.

What it is built to do

Make it RAIN is not an app builder and not a promise of revenue. It is a path from "I shipped this" toward "someone might actually pay," using:

  • First Customer Path
  • Founder Brief
  • Buyer Stress Test
  • Buyer hypothesis and paid problem
  • Smallest paid offer and a price to test
  • The next conversation worth having

Do not automate a guess. If the buyer, problem, or offer is still assumed, more ads and more features will not fix that.
