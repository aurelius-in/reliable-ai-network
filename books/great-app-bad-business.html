Who it is for

App builders, technical founders, indie hackers, SaaS founders, AI-assisted builders, and developers who have shipped or nearly shipped something real, with weak or uncertain revenue.

What the book is actually about

AI made software dramatically easier to build. It did not make people care, trust, buy, pay, or stay. The work is finding where the path to payment is breaking, not adding another feature to a product nobody has a reason to purchase.

Part I

The Product Isn't the Business

Shipping is the starting line. Usage is not payment. A feature request is not demand. Find where the money is breaking before you build again.

Part II

Who May Actually Pay?

Persona is not demand. Pain is not enough. "I'd use this" is not a purchase. The market gets a vote, including whether you can reach the buyer.

Part III

Make the App Buyable

The app is not the offer. Price is a hypothesis. Trust is a separate constraint. Create a purchase moment instead of hoping interest turns into a decision.

Part IV

Get to the First Customer

Distribution is not posting. Follow the trust path. Pick the next conversation worth an hour, then write a first-customer path a buyer can actually walk.

Part V

Let Buyers Teach You the Business

An objection is data. Ask why this person paid. If they pay but do not stay, that is a different failure than never getting paid at all.
