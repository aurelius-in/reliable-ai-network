Where the path breaks

Answer in order. Observed means behavior you could show someone else. Unknown is first-class. Later “observed” stays locked until the earlier step has evidence.

You cannot mark a later step observed until the earlier one has evidence. If you have never asked anyone to pay, you cannot pass “values but will not pay.”

1. Can you identify plausible buyers?

People you can name or describe by observable conditions, who own the consequence.

2. Do those buyers reply when you reach them?

Replies, not opens.

3. Will they undertake a real evaluation?

Against a real case, with their own data or workflow.

4. Do they experience meaningful value?

The promised outcome occurred, in their terms.

5. Will they pay under real terms?

The standard offer at the standard price, not a favor.

6. After paying, do they stay?

Activation, continued use, renewal.
