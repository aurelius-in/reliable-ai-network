Make it RAIN

You built something real. Now it's time to get paid.

Make it RAIN is for software creators who have shipped an app, SaaS product, AI tool, or working MVP, but still have unanswered commercial questions.

It helps a builder investigate questions such as:

  • Who may actually pay?
  • What problem is economically important enough to buy?
  • What is the smallest paid offer worth testing?
  • What price should be tested?
  • What remains assumption rather than evidence?
  • Who is the next person worth talking to?

First Customer Path, Founder Brief, Buyer Stress Test, buyer hypothesis, paid problem, smallest paid offer, price to test, and the next conversation worth having. Do not automate a guess.
